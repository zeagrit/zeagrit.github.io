What we do

From the Japanese secondary market to your wrist.

Sourced in Japan

We buy directly from the Japanese secondary market, known worldwide for well-kept, high-quality pre-owned items.

Inspected before listing

Every watch is checked before it is listed, and mechanical watches are serviced when needed. Condition is described honestly.

Professional photography

All items are photographed by a professional studio partner, so you see exactly what you will receive.

Fast worldwide shipping

Orders are carefully packed and shipped internationally with DHL Express and other trusted carriers.
